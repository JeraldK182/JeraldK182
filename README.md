## About Me

I am a **Systems Engineer** focused on infrastructure, cloud operations, technical support, and dependable service delivery. I support systems, networks, end users, and client environments across a range of operational requirements.

My experience covers Level 1–5 technical support, Windows administration, Active Directory, network configuration, CCTV provisioning, telephony support, infrastructure upgrades, remote support, and data-centre operations. I approach technology with a practical, service-focused mindset: understand the issue, communicate clearly, and deliver reliable outcomes.
